### Latent Geometry as a Structural Monitor: Eigenspace Alignment for Anomaly Detection in Anonymity Networks

**Author:** Vaibhav Chhabra
**Published:** May 19, 2026
**arXiv:** [2605.20391](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20391)
**Patent:** USPTO #64/034,500 — Filed April 9, 2026

A dual-observer geometric framework for detecting structural change in anonymity
networks using only public relay metadata. Validated across 67 consecutive daily
observation windows on the Tor network. Primary detection gates achieve 0.0% false
positive rate. The February 20, 2026 Cloudflare BGP withdrawal is formally
documented as a confirmed infrastructure event.
